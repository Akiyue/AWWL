\section*{Data availability}
All datasets used in this study are public: CIFAR-10
\citep{krizhevsky2009learning}, CelebA \citep{liu2015deep} at $64\times64$,
and one subject of the DreamBooth dataset \citep{ruiz2023dreambooth}. The
repository \url{https://github.com/Akiyue/AWWL} releases the source code
(training, evaluation, table generation and plotting), the experiment
configurations and the per-run metric ledgers from which every table, figure
and number in the text is generated.
Model weights and generated
samples are not included because of their size; they can be regenerated from
the released configurations and seeds.

During the preparation of this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) in
