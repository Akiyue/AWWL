A learned weighting is the natural objection to \S\ref{sec:confound}: perhaps
the hand-designed schedule is simply the wrong one. We therefore also train
objectives whose weights are learned rather than specified---an
uncertainty-weighted variant conditioned on $\sigma$, which removes $\alpha$
and $p$ entirely, and a GradNorm balancer over the sub-bands. Either outcome is
informative. A learned weighting that improves the spectrum further while FID
stays flat confirms the accounting of \S\ref{sec:worth} on an independent
mechanism; one that succeeds would be this paper's positive result.
